# BuildEstimate Pro 🏗️

> **Mobile-First Construction Material Takeoff & Project Cost Estimator (Progressive Web App)**  
> Runs seamlessly on **iOS, Android, and Desktop**. Installable directly to home screens without an app store.

---

## 🌟 Overview
**BuildEstimate Pro** is an open-source, mobile-first construction estimation suite tailored for general contractors, project managers, construction estimators, and Virtual Assistants.

It simplifies job-site takeoffs into instant, real-time calculations:
- **Concrete & Slab Takeoff:** Calculate cubic yards, ready-mix truck counts, and rebar grid requirements (#4 rebar) with custom waste factors.
- **Wall Framing & Drywall Takeoff:** Calculate stud counts (16" or 24" OC), plates, drywall sheets (4x8 and 4x12), screws, joint compound, and tape with door/window deductions.
- **Paint & Surface Coatings:** Calculate finish coats, primer, 5-gallon buckets, and 1-gallon cans.
- **Master Project Proposal & Contractor Markup:** Synthesize material costs, labor hours, subcontractor bids, equipment, contingency reserves, and contractor profit margins into an executive client quote.
- **PDF Export / Print:** Generates a professional, print-ready Bill of Quantities (BOQ) with client signature line.
- **Local Storage:** Automatically saves recent estimates on your device for offline reference.

---

## 📱 Mobile App (PWA) Features
- **Android:** 1-tap "Install App" prompt saves to your home screen as a native standalone app.
- **iOS (iPhone/iPad):** Open in Safari $\rightarrow$ Tap Share $\rightarrow$ "Add to Home Screen".
- **Offline Capable:** Powered by Service Workers (`sw.js`) and Web App Manifest (`manifest.json`).

---

## 💻 Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** HTML5, Modern Tailwind CSS
- **App Platform:** Progressive Web App (PWA)
- **Zero Dependencies:** Pure vanilla JS logic for instant 60 FPS mobile responsiveness.
