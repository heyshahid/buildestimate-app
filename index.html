Civil Engineering Formulas & Technical Field Manual

Mathematical derivations for nominal mix ratios, rebar clearances, masonry brick factors, and financial markups.

1.54 Dry Volume Factor (Concrete)

Dry Volume = Wet Volume × 1.54
Cement Bags = (Ratio_Part / Sum_Ratios) × Dry_Vol / 1.25 CFT
Sand Volume = (Sand_Part / Sum_Ratios) × Dry_Vol
Aggregate = (Agg_Part / Sum_Ratios) × Dry_Vol

Accounts for 30-35% void space between coarse aggregates and 20% void space in sand when water is mixed.

Contractor Markup vs Profit Margin

Markup on Cost: Price = Cost × (1 + Markup%)
Margin on Price: Price = Cost / (1 - Margin%)
Example: 20% Margin on $10,000 cost = $12,500 (Profit: $2,500)

A 20% markup yields only a 16.67% true margin. BuildEstimate Pro allows toggling both to protect contractor margins.
